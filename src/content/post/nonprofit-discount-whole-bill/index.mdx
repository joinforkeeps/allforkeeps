Ten free licences. A worthy mission. A platform used by large organisations.

It sounds like the expensive part is over.

Usually, you have only finished reading the first line of the bill.

## Start with the facts, not the sales pitch

Salesforce's Power of Us programme offers eligible organisations ten donated Enterprise Edition licences and discounts on additional licences, products, and services. Its April 27, 2026 guidance lists Agentforce Nonprofit and Sales and Service bundles.

That is a real benefit. It is not the same as a complete, configured, maintained nonprofit system at no cost.

Someone still has to design the records, import the data, train the team, manage permissions, and keep the integrations working.

Your eleventh teammate is not automatically a penalty. But growing beyond a donation can change your budget. Get the actual quote before you build the next three years around an assumption.

## NPSP has not lost support

This matters enough to say plainly.

Salesforce says the Nonprofit Success Pack remains fully supported. It calls the end of direct feature development “End of Innovation.” It also says existing NPSP organisations are not required to migrate to Agentforce Nonprofit until they are ready.

Support, product development, and migration pressure are different questions. Mixing them together makes a punchier accusation and a worse decision.

You do not need an inaccurate claim to ask whether the platform still fits your organisation.

## Three changes worth remembering

Salesforce moved the Power of Us Hub into the broader Trailblazer Community in November 2021.

Microsoft ended the donated Microsoft 365 Business Premium and Office 365 E1 offers at renewals on or after July 1, 2025, while retaining Business Basic grants and discounted alternatives.

Google completed the Currents shutdown in 2023 and began deleting remaining data on August 8 that year.

Three different changes: a community migration, a grant retirement, and a service shutdown.

Not one interchangeable scandal. One very practical lesson: the terms around an external dependency can change.

## The fundraising rebrand that was not a shutdown

Classy became GoFundMe Pro in 2025. The company's transition FAQ said existing plans, pricing, and fees did not change because of that transition.

Call it a rebrand, not a killed product.

Then do the useful work: read your contract, distinguish platform fees from payment-processing fees, and find out what a donor-covered fee actually covers. A fee disclosed in a contract is not automatically hidden. It can still be a cost you should compare.

## Buy the outcome, not the impressive feature list

At For Keeps, we want organisations to understand the whole operating model before committing.

That includes our model. Free learning materials. Paid implementation and optional premium work. Infrastructure and outside services with their own costs. Open source licenses that still have conditions.

No mystery. No claim that maintenance performs itself.
