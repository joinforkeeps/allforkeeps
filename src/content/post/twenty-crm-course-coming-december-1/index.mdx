**Registration is not open yet.** The learning site is being prepared for two
separate courses, planned for December 1:

1. **Course 1: Open-source software and self-hosting**, a free introduction for anyone
   who wants to learn.
2. **Course 2: Twenty CRM**, a nonprofit-focused course with a planned price
   of ${pricingConfig.coursePriceUsd} {pricingConfig.currency} one time. Paid
   registration is not live.

The course is delivered through [our learning site](https://learn.allforkeeps.com/lms/courses).
Until registration is confirmed live, the right next step is to
[join the mailing list](https://news.allforkeeps.com/subscription/form), not to
look for a checkout button.

## One substitution at a time

The Twenty CRM course is designed around a small, testable workflow. Start
with sample contacts, learn what the system needs to run, and understand who
will maintain it before any real organisation data moves.

The course plan includes an open source introduction, a self-hosting
introduction, Docker setup instructions, and a starter pack. The free
introduction and reference material stay accessible. Help with setup and
using the system are reserved for assessment clients.

## What is not live

Checkout and paid registration are not available yet. We will not ask anyone
to pay or imply that a course can already be enrolled in. The December 1 date
is a plan, not a guarantee of payment availability.

If you are evaluating donor software now, start with sample data, write down
the workflow your team needs, and review the maintenance and exit plan before
you make a commitment.
